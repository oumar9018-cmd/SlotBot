Pricing questions

Is there really a free plan?
Yes — 10 bookings per month, free forever, no credit card. It's perfect for tutors just starting out or testing the waters.
What counts as a booking?
One booked session = one booking, whether it's paid or free. Cancelled sessions don't count against your limit.
How do payments work on Pro?
Students pay via UPI, cards or netbanking when they book. Money settles directly to your bank account. SlotTutor charges zero commission on your earnings.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. Monthly Pro cancels in one click and you keep Pro until the month ends. Annual plans are refundable in the first 14 days.
