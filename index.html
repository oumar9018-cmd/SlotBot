Frequently asked

Is SlotBot really free to start?
Yes. The Free plan includes 10 bookings per month with no credit card required. Upgrade to Pro (₹249/month) only when you need unlimited bookings and instant payments.
How do students pay me?
Students pay online at the moment they book, using UPI, cards or netbanking. The money goes directly to your account — SlotBot never holds your earnings.
What if a student cancels?
You set your own cancellation policy. If you cancel a session, the student is refunded automatically — no disputes, no manual work.
Do I need any technical skills?
None at all. Sign in with Google, set your hours, share your link. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use SlotBot.
Can I use it for group classes?
SlotBot v1 is built for 1:1 sessions, which is where solo tutors earn the most per hour. Group classes are on our roadmap.
